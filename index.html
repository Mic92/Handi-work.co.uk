About Handi-Work

A family business built on word of mouth

Handi-Work is run by Marks Lekwati, a time-served tradesman based in Leith with hands-on experience across Edinburgh's tenements, terraces and new builds. Most of our work comes from repeat customers and their recommendations — which is exactly how we like it.

Because one person sees the job through from strip-out to snag list, nothing gets lost between trades: the tiling lines up with the joinery, the paint goes on after the dust is gone, and you always know who to call.
